Card on File Terms & Disclosures

1. Authorization

By checking the authorization box and selecting "Save my card securely," you authorize Bross Inc., doing business as Ozark Cleaners and Laundry ("Ozark Cleaners," "we," "us"), to keep the payment card you enter on file and to charge it for cleaning, laundry, alteration, and related services you order from us — including orders placed in store, by phone, or through our free pickup & delivery route. Charge amounts will vary with the services you purchase and will match the prices shown on your ticket, invoice, or account statement. This authorization remains in effect until you revoke it or we remove the card.

2. How your card information is protected

Your card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Fiserv (CardPointe®), inside the secure entry form above — they are transmitted to the processor over an encrypted connection. Ozark Cleaners never sees, receives, or stores your full card number or security code. The processor converts your card into a secure token; we retain only that token, the card brand, and the last four digits so we can recognize your card. Card processing is protected under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

3. No charge today

Saving your card does not charge it. Your bank may briefly show a $0.00 verification, which is not a charge and disappears on its own.

4. Receipts & statements

A receipt is available for every charge at pickup or delivery, and account customers receive an itemized statement. You may request a copy of any receipt, invoice, or statement at either store.

5. Declined or expired cards

If a charge is declined or your card expires, your balance remains due, and we may contact you for an updated card or another form of payment.

6. Removing your card

You may remove your card or revoke this authorization at any time by calling either store or telling a team member. Revocation applies to future charges and does not cancel amounts already owed for services performed.

7. Electronic consent

Checking the authorization box and saving your card acts as your electronic signature and agreement to these terms. You may print or save this page for your records; a copy of these terms is available at either store on request.
