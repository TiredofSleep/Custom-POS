One-Time Payment Terms & Disclosures

1. Authorization

By checking the authorization box and selecting "Pay securely," you authorize Bross Inc., doing business as Ozark Cleaners and Laundry ("Ozark Cleaners," "we," "us"), to charge the payment card you enter one time, for the amount shown above, as payment toward the balance on your account for cleaning, laundry, alteration, and related services you have already received from us.

2. How your card information is protected

Your card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Fiserv (CardPointe®), inside the secure entry form above — they are transmitted to the processor over an encrypted connection. Ozark Cleaners never sees, receives, or stores your full card number or security code. We keep only the card brand, the last four digits, and the processor's transaction reference, so we can record your payment. Card processing is protected under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

3. One charge only — your card is not saved

This page charges your card once and does not keep your card on file or authorize any future charges. If you would like a card on file for future orders, ask us for a separate card-on-file link.

4. Receipts & statements

This payment appears on your account history and statement, and a receipt is available at either store on request.

5. Declined cards

If your card is declined, nothing is charged and your balance remains due. You may try another card, or pay in store or by phone.

6. Questions, errors & disputes

If you believe the amount is wrong or you were charged in error, call either store and we will review your account and correct any error. You may also contact the bank that issued your card.

7. This link

This link can be paid once and expires 7 days after it was sent. If your balance changes before you pay — for example, if you pay in store — the link stops working so you are never charged twice; call us for an updated link.

8. Electronic consent

Checking the authorization box and paying acts as your electronic signature and agreement to these terms. You may print or save this page for your records; a copy of these terms is available at either store on request.
